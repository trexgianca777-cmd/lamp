SYNTHETIC SAMPLE LEASE. Not a real lease. Written for demo purposes only.

Landlord may increase monthly rent upon thirty (30) days' written notice, with no cap stated in this agreement.

This lease renews automatically for twelve (12) months unless Tenant gives ninety (90) days' written notice before expiry.

Tenant must keep the yard mowed and gutters clear; failure is a lease violation.

Security deposit of $1,200 will be returned within 30 days of move-out, minus deductions for damages beyond normal wear.

Guests staying more than 7 consecutive nights require Landlord's written approval.

Early termination requires 60 days' notice plus a fee equal to two months' rent.
